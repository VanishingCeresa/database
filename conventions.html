Conventions

  • Chow and Griffiths groups are taken with $\mathbb{Q}$-coefficients, so "vanishes" means "is torsion".
  • Every example is a non-hyperelliptic curve, or a family of them, with torsion Ceresa cycle.
  • Chow group torsion modulo rational equivalence (so also in the Griffiths group).
  • Griffiths group torsion modulo algebraic equivalence.
  • Conditional torsion assuming a conjecture, named in the entry.
  • 3.48-33.2-3-12 A label is genus, group identifier, signature. The group identifier is the GAP id $[48,33]$ of the automorphism group, and the signature lists the orders of the branch points of $C \to C/\mathrm{Aut}(C)$.
  • The $\mathrm{Aut}(C)$ column shows the group's symbol when it has one ($C_n$ is the cyclic group of order $n$), and its GAP id otherwise.
