Vanishing locus of the Ceresa cycle

The Ceresa cycle of a curve $C$ is a natural algebraic cycle on its Jacobian: the difference between $C$ and its image under multiplication by $-1$. It is always homologically trivial, yet Ceresa proved that it is not torsion for a very general curve of genus at least $3$. For hyperelliptic curves it vanishes, because the hyperelliptic involution acts as $-1$ on the Jacobian. Beyond these, the first non-hyperelliptic curves with torsion Ceresa cycle were found only a few years ago. This site collects the known examples, with how each is proved and where, so that anyone can use them, in the hope of better understanding the torsion locus of the Ceresa cycle outside the hyperelliptic locus.
