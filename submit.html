Fill in the form on GitHub (a free account is needed). A maintainer checks it, gives it a label and adds it to the catalogue.

Submit a curve

What to include

  • Equation, field of definition, genus and automorphism group
  • A proof that the curve is non-hyperelliptic (how to test)
  • Where the Ceresa cycle is torsion: Chow group, Griffiths group, or conditionally (say on which conjecture)
  • How it is proved, the methods used, who did it, and references

No GitHub account? Email the same information to vanishing_ceresa@proton.me.
