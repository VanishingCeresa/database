LS and QZ vanishing criteria

Throughout, $C$ is a smooth projective curve of genus $g \ge 2$ over an algebraically closed field, $J$ is its Jacobian, and $\kappa(C)$ is its Ceresa cycle, taken with $\mathbb{Q}$-coefficients. Both criteria are stated in terms of the action of $\mathrm{Aut}(C)$ on cohomology.

Laga–Shnidman

Criterion A (Laga–Shnidman). If $H^3(J)^{\mathrm{Aut}(C)} = 0$, then $\kappa(C)$ is torsion modulo rational equivalence. [LS24, Theorem A]

Criterion B (Laga–Shnidman). Assume the Hodge conjecture. If $H^0(J, \Omega^3_J)^{\mathrm{Aut}(C)} = 0$, then $\kappa(C)$ is torsion modulo algebraic equivalence. [LS24, Theorem B]
